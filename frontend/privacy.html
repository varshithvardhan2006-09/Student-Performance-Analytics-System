Privacy Policy

This is a student project. This page explains what the application stores and why.

What is stored

  • Teacher account details: full name, email address and a bcrypt hash of the password. The password itself is never stored.
  • The marks data a teacher uploads: roll numbers, student names, marks and the analytics calculated from them.
  • A record of which reports were downloaded and when.

Uploaded files

Uploaded files are read in memory and are not saved to disk. Only the extracted records and results are stored in the database.

AI features

If the server has a Groq API key configured, summaries of class statistics, a student's question, or the text of an uploaded PDF may be sent to Groq to produce insights. Without a key, nothing is sent anywhere and local summaries are used.

Who can see the data

Each teacher can only see their own uploads. Data is not sold or shared. Cookies are not used; the login token is kept in your browser's session storage and the optional "remember me" email in local storage.

Deleting data

Deleting an upload from the History page removes its students, analytics, insights and report records. To delete your account, contact the person running this installation.
